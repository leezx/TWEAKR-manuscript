# Fig. 1b legend (draft)

**b, Predicted developmental potential across epithelial, immune and stromal compartments of treatment-naive primary CRC.**

**Left panel.**

- Each point is one patient: the median CytoTRACE2 score of that patient's cells in the compartment (*n* = 222 patients from 13 cohorts; 1,000,926 cells).
- Per-sample medians were computed first and then summarised per patient as the unweighted median across samples. A patient × compartment needed ≥30 cells.
- Boxes show the median and interquartile range of patient medians; whiskers extend to 1.5 × IQR.
- Open diamonds show the median of patient medians within each cohort.

**Right panel.**

- The plotted value is the pooled within-patient difference in patient-median score between compartments (95% confidence interval).
- Pooling: study-specific mean differences were combined by random-effects meta-analysis (REML with the Hartung–Knapp adjustment). The three contrasts were Holm-corrected as one family (all adjusted *P* < 0.001).
- Filled circles: original counts (*n* = 222 patients).
- Open circles: sequencing-depth-matched counts, with cells downsampled to a study-specific target depth (*n* = 220 patients). They show the mean estimate and mean 95% CI across five downsampling seeds; seed-to-seed range ≤0.003.
- The three contrasts were pooled separately, so their estimates need not sum exactly.
- The dashed line marks no difference.

**Notes.**

- The MUI Innsbruck cohort, profiled using BD Rhapsody, showed a larger epithelial–immune difference than the other cohorts. Excluding this study did not materially alter the pooled estimate (leave-one-study-out range 0.071–0.077).
- Depth matching attenuated all three differences by approximately 43–55% while preserving their direction.
- CytoTRACE2 scores are model predictions of transcriptional developmental potential, not measurements of cellular plasticity.

Study-specific estimates, 95% prediction intervals and all sensitivity analyses are shown in Extended Data. Two pre-specified sensitivity analyses (permissive eligibility 20 cells/5 patients; HTAPP–HTAN) were not performed.
