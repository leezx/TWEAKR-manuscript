# Extended Data Fig. (Fig. 1b companion) legend (draft)

**Cohort heterogeneity, robustness and cell-type resolution of CytoTRACE2-predicted developmental potential.**

**a, Cohort-specific within-patient differences for the three compartment contrasts.**

- Squares show cohort means with 95% *t*-intervals. The number of patients is in parentheses.
- Diamonds show the random-effects pooled estimate with its 95% confidence interval (REML with Hartung–Knapp adjustment).
- Grey bars show the 95% prediction interval, i.e. the expected range for a new cohort.
- The dashed line marks no difference; the dotted line marks the pooled estimate.

**b, Pooled within-patient differences (95% CI) across the completed sensitivity analyses.**

- The number of patients is in parentheses.
- Depth-matched analyses downsample cells to a study-specific target depth. They show the mean estimate and mean CI across five seeds.
- Raw model score is the per-cell model prediction before CytoTRACE2 smoothing and rank binning.
- Detection-adjusted estimates are per-cohort regression intercepts at zero difference in detected model genes. This is a secondary, exploratory adjustment.
- Low-gene cells are cells with fewer than 500 detected model genes.
- Two pre-specified analyses were not performed: permissive eligibility (20 cells/5 patients) and HTAPP–HTAN.

**c, Patient-level CytoTRACE2 scores for the harmonized L2 cell types.**

- A patient enters a type with ≥30 cells. Numbers above each box give eligible patients and cohorts.
- Boxes show the median and IQR; whiskers extend to 1.5 × IQR.
- *Malignant epi., high-confidence malignant epithelial cells (CNV-based). This is a conservative, MSS-enriched subset from 8 cohorts and does not represent the whole epithelial compartment.
- Dendritic cells are mainly cDC2.
- This panel is descriptive. Cross-lineage differences may reflect lineage-specific transcriptional programmes rather than differences in functional plasticity.

**d, Cohort × L2 type median of patient-level scores.**

- Hatched cells: no patient in that cohort met the ≥30-cell eligibility for the type.
