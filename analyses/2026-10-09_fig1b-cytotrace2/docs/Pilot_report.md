# Fig. 1B CytoTRACE2 pilot — technical report (contract A2.1)

- **Date:** 2026-10-09
- **Status:** for the user's final technical review. The full 13-study run stays on HOLD until approval.
- **Scope:** Joanito, Lee, Qin; 165 samples; 421,649 cells; CytoTRACE2 1.1.0; Argos SGE `all.q`.
- **Content:** technical diagnostics only. No compartment contrast, and no direction of any contrast, is
  reported or was inspected for this report. `pilot_potency_shares.csv` was generated by the script but
  is not interpreted. Review decision (2026-10-09): the file is kept in the repository as a
  reproducibility record. It is excluded from the technical review, and A2.1 is not changed because of
  its values.
- **Naming:** "raw score" means the per-cell model output (`preprocessData` + `predictData`; code
  columns `intrinsic_*`). It is not called intrinsic plasticity.
- **Tables:** all numbers come from `tables/pilot/`. The Argos source is `pilot/summary/`.

## 1. Raw-score invariance

**Test:** the 150 target cells per compartment from one sample (Lee KUL21_T) were scored alone and
then again with 3,000 added background cells. The background was epithelial only, immune only,
stromal only, or all three (`pilot_invariance_summary.csv`).

| Score | Effect of the added background cells |
|---|---|
| Raw score | **identical**: max \|Δ\| = 0 in all 12 conditions |
| Final whole-run score | median \|Δ\| 0.009–0.052; max \|Δ\| 0.05–0.26 |

**Potency-category agreement for the final score:**
- Immune 0.99–1.00
- Epithelial 0.99–1.00
- Stromal 0.77–0.88

**Model-gene-only export:** scores were identical to an all-gene input on one sample (max \|Δ\| = 0
for all three modes; `pilot_allgenes_equivalence.csv`).

**Reading:** this confirms the source-code finding. The raw score is per-cell. The final score depends
on which other cells are in the run, most strongly in stromal cells. The raw-score sensitivity
analysis stays mandatory, as in the contract.

## 2. Whole-sample vs compartment-split runs

Spearman correlations between run modes, per study × compartment (`pilot_mode_agreement.csv`):

| Compartment | ρ whole vs split | ρ whole vs raw | Potency agreement, whole vs raw |
|---|---|---|---|
| Epithelial | 0.64–0.73 | 0.65–0.71 | 0.76–0.85 |
| Immune | 0.37–0.61 | 0.30–0.56 | 0.95–0.99 |
| Stromal | 0.86–0.91 | 0.80–0.85 | 0.55–0.72 |

**Between-compartment spread:** the range of the three compartment medians within a sample was compared
between split and whole runs. The comparison is unsigned (`pilot_split_compression.csv`, 164
samples):

| Study | Split / whole ratio, median (IQR) |
|---|---|
| Joanito | 0.91 (0.70–0.96) |
| Lee | 0.68 (0.31–0.85) |
| Qin | 0.62 (0.40–0.73) |

For the raw score, the ratio to the whole-run range was 0.84–0.99.

**Reading:**
- Splitting by compartment compresses the differences between compartments, because of the
  within-run rank uniformisation.
- Agreement with the whole-sample run is poor in immune cells.
- This supports the contract: the whole sample is the primary run unit, and split runs are a
  diagnostic only.

## 3. Depth matching and seed stability

**Depth matching:** cells were downsampled to exactly T_s, with 5 seeds
(`pilot_matched_cell_depth.csv`, `pilot_depth_dependence.csv`).

- **Selection effect alone** (keeping only the cells that reach T_s, without downsampling, vs the full
  run on the same cells):
  - per-cell ρ 0.78–0.97;
  - median shift ≤ 0.006.
- **Depth effect** (downsampled, mean of the 5 seeds, vs selection-only, on the same cells):

  | Compartment | Final score: median \|shift\| | Final score: ρ | Raw score: median \|shift\| | Raw score: ρ |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Epithelial | 0.021–0.051 | 0.69–0.80 | 0.027–0.057 | 0.87–0.89 |
  | Immune | ≤ 0.003 | 0.24–0.68 | ≤ 0.0003 | 0.88–0.90 |
  | Stromal | **0.12–0.17** | 0.68–0.87 | **0.11–0.16** | 0.82–0.89 |

  - The low immune ρ for the final score reflects a narrow score range, not large shifts.
  - The signed shifts are kept in the table and are not interpreted here.
- **Correlation of score with detected model genes, within study × compartment:**
  - before matching: ρ 0.11–0.65;
  - after matching: \|ρ\| ≤ 0.32, with mixed signs.
- **Seed stability:**
  - SD of patient medians across the 5 seeds: median ≤ 0.006 in every study × compartment
    (`pilot_depth_matched_seed_spread.csv`);
  - the precision of patient medians from cell bootstrap was ≤ 0.006 in epithelial and immune and
    0.036–0.061 in stromal, so seed noise is small relative to sampling noise.

**Reading:**
- Sequencing depth has a material effect, which is largest in stromal cells and affects the raw score
  equally.
- The depth-matched sensitivity analysis is therefore informative and should stay as specified.
- Five seeds are sufficient.

## 4. Sample-wise vs patient-pooled runs

**Data:** 39 patients with more than one sample (Joanito 29, Lee 10;
`pilot_sample_vs_patient_pool.csv`). Every Qin patient has one sample, so this check is not evaluable
for Qin.

- **Per-cell agreement:** median ρ 0.92–0.94 (minimum 0.66); median per-cell \|Δ\| 0.011.
- **Patient-median \|Δ\|, median:**
  - Epithelial 0.001–0.002
  - Immune < 0.001
  - Stromal 0.006–0.012
- **Patient-median \|Δ\|, maximum:**
  - Epithelial 0.066
  - Immune 0.010
  - Stromal 0.052

**Batching (> 10,000 cells):** one Qin sample (11,345 cells) was run 4 times with different seeds
(`pilot_batching_repeats.csv`). The per-cell SD of the final score had a median of 0.011 and a 95th
percentile of 0.052.

**Reading:** the choice of run unit changes patient medians only slightly. The sample remains the
primary run unit.

## 5. Low-gene cells (< 500 detected model genes)

Cells with < 500 detected model genes were dropped after scoring, and patient medians were
recomputed (`pilot_low_gene_effect.csv`).

| Study | % cells < 500 model genes (Epi / Imm / Str) | Median \|Δ\| in patient medians (Epi / Imm / Str) | ρ of patient medians |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joanito | 0 / 5.1 / 0 | 0 / < 0.001 / 0 | 0.84–1.00 |
| Lee | 18.8 / 12.2 / 8.4 | 0.002 / 0.001 / 0.002 | 0.95–0.97 |
| Qin | 23.3 / 41.9 / 20.2 | 0.005 / 0.008 / **0.044** | 0.77–0.92 |

- No patient lost eligibility (≥ 30 cells per compartment).
- The effect is concentrated in Qin, a lower-depth study, and is largest in Qin stromal cells.
- The "< 500 model genes" sensitivity analysis is therefore relevant to the low-depth studies (Qin,
  Qian, Qi), and should be run as specified.
