# Extended Data Fig. (Fig. 1c robustness) — legend draft (2026-10-11)

Wording follows the frozen claim (`docs/Fig1c_stage4_final_review_20261011.md`). The ED figure number is assigned at manuscript assembly.

**Annotation, programme architecture and robustness of the association between epithelial programme strata and predicted developmental potency.**

- **a, Lineage transfer audit (ED-A).**
  - Left: per-study marker-module audit of the lineage classes transferred from the Pelka et al. reference by the patient-grouped cross-validated classifier. A class is "verified" when its marker module exceeds the comparator cells as pre-specified. "Failed (unverified)" classes are kept but flagged lineage-transfer-unverified; "not evaluable" means too few cells.
  - Middle: annotation composition of all tumour-specimen-derived epithelial cells per study after the out-of-distribution (OOD) amendment v0.3a. Transitional and rare classes were not module-audited.
  - Right: fraction of cells failing the original OOD check, by study (the reason for amendment v0.3a).
- **b, Programme architecture on the raw scale (ED-B).**
  - Left: raw UCell proCSC and revCSC scores (50 × 50 bins; each patient weighted equally). Hue = stratum holding most of the bin's weight; opacity = bin density (log scale). Strata are defined within each sample, so no global cut-off exists on the raw scale.
  - Right: membership of the full-signature p75 strata (rows) under the mechanism-blinded signatures (columns), as % of each row; J = Jaccard index. Reference cells (27,976) are identical under both and are omitted.
  - Stratum sizes (pro-dominant / rev-dominant / hybrid-high cells): p70 54,881 / 49,633 / 29,876; p75 50,539 / 46,048 / 20,334; p80 44,357 / 40,687 / 12,479.
- **c, Study-level estimates for the three primary contrasts (ED-C).** Squares, study mean of patient-level within-sample differences in CytoTRACE2 score (size ∝ patients) ± 1.96 SE. Diamond, REML random-effects pooled estimate with Hartung–Knapp 95% CI; grey line, 95% prediction interval (PI). I², τ² and the range of leave-one-study-out (LOSO) pooled estimates are shown in each panel. The pro − rev PI crosses 0: the average effect is positive in all 13 studies, but its direction in a new cohort is not guaranteed.
- **d, Depth and transcriptomic complexity (ED-D).** Final (primary) and raw CytoTRACE2 scores; contrasts adjusted within sample for log UMI, log genes, mitochondrial fraction, S and G2M scores (full) or a reduced covariate set; mean of five depth-matched (downsampled) rescorings. D2: A′, final score on the same samples; B, final score restricted to cells retained in all five downsampled replicates; C, downsampled score on those cells. B ≈ A′ while C is attenuated, so the attenuation reflects rescoring at lower depth rather than cell selection.
- **e, Stratum and reference definitions (ED-E).** p70 and p80 strata; strata defined with mechanism-blinded signatures (genes of the frozen mechanism set removed: TNFRSF12A, the YAP core genes, TNFSF12, NFKB2, RELB, TRAF1 and BIRC3); strict reference and broad reference (absorptive + secretory; secretory cells leave the pro/rev strata, which changes pro − rev); the three reference definitions compared on a common patient subset; OOD-pass cells only. The strict reference and common-subset rows apply only to contrasts that use the reference.
- **f, Molecular and cohort subsets (ED-F).** MSS and MSI-H tumours; cells assigned to the Atlas "Malignant epithelial" (CNV-positive) class; all patients with Pelka et al. excluded. Caption line: study-matched MSI-H − MSS interaction for pro − rev in the four studies with ≥ 5 patients of each status.
- **g, Potency proxy (ED-G).** Left: categorical contrasts estimated with SCENT-CCAT instead of CytoTRACE2 (proxy units differ; compare direction, not magnitude). Right: continuous model (within-sample partial Spearman ρ adjusted for the other programme, technical covariates and lineage/annotation status) for both proxies, 216 patients in 13 studies.
- **h, Eligibility and reference missingness (ED-H).** Left: patients retained at each eligibility step (all patients; with both groups present; ≥ 1 eligible sample; in studies with ≥ 5 eligible patients). Middle (D1): difference between cells lost and cells retained on depth matching, pooled across studies (95% CI); reference cells lost (median 13.9% per sample) have lower depth but the same CytoTRACE2 score. Right (D3): patients losing eligibility in the intersection analysis, by patient-level revCSC tertile.
- **Notes.**
  - Unless stated otherwise, points and bars are REML random-effects pooled estimates with Hartung–Knapp 95% CIs; the unit of analysis is the patient, nested in study. "k = …, desc." marks rows with fewer than 3 studies, which were reported descriptively only. Arrows mark intervals truncated at the axis limit.
  - Only the three primary contrasts in Fig. 1c form the confirmatory (Holm) family; all analyses shown here are sensitivity analyses. They were pre-specified, but their implementation details were documented after the primary analysis.
  - CytoTRACE2 and SCENT-CCAT estimate transcriptome-derived developmental potency and do not measure plasticity. Strata are operational, programme-defined groups, not functionally validated cancer stem cell populations; tumour-specimen-derived epithelium is not exclusively malignant.
