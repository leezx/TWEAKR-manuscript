# CP1D_L2_tweak_associated_state — legend (checkpoint figure)

**Maternal decidual macrophages scoring high for an AOM/DSS-derived Tnfsf12+ TAM core program
(GPNMB, SPP1, TREM2, APOE, FABP5, PLD3) show modestly higher detection of TNFSF12.** TNFSF12 is
an independent readout and is not part of any score.

- **a**: CD14_M + CD16_M nuclei (n = 9,276) on the zoomed author-integrated UMAP, coloured by
  core score (2nd–98th percentile). Open red circles mark the 212 TNFSF12-detected nuclei.
- **b**: Within each donor with ≥40 macrophages, nuclei were split at the donor median core
  score. Lines connect the % TNFSF12-detected in the low and high halves of the same donor.
  The Mantel–Haenszel OR is pooled over the 14 donors with ≥1 TNFSF12+ macrophage.
- **c**: OR per SD of module score from logistic regression with donor fixed effects and
  log10 library size. Informative donors only; 95% Wald CI. Shown for all maternal
  macrophages and for CD14_M only.
- **d**: OR per SD for 500 random 6-gene modules, each gene matched to the expression bin of
  its core gene; the line marks the observed core OR. Empirical one-sided P.

n refers to nuclei within 14 informative donors. Nuclei are not independent; donor-level
summaries are given in `tables/cp1d/`. The C1Q complement control module shows a comparable
association (panel c), so the signal is not specific to the core program.
