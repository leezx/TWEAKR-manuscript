# CP1D_L1_immune_landscape — legend (checkpoint figure)

**Immune compartment of the human maternal–fetal interface snRNA-seq atlas (Wang et al.,
Nature 2026; 23 donors, GW5–39), using author cell-type labels.**

- **a**: Author-integrated UMAP restricted to 23,938 immune nuclei, coloured by author label.
  The key gives nuclei counts.
- **b**: Marker detection. Dot area shows the percentage of nuclei with UMI > 0; colour shows
  the mean per-nucleus log1p(CP10k).
- **c**: Immune composition per donor, ordered by gestational week.

There is no separate monocyte label, and mast cells are essentially absent. Descriptive figure; no statistics.
