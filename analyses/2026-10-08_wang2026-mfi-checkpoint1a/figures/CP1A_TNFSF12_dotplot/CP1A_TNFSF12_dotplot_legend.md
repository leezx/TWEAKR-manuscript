# CP1A_TNFSF12_dotplot — legend (checkpoint QC figure, not a manuscript panel)

**TNFSF12 expression across annotated cell types of the human maternal–fetal interface
(Wang et al., Nature 2026; snRNA-seq, 193,202 nuclei, 23 donors, GW5–39).** Rows show the
36 author cell-type labels, grouped by the authors' cell-type-level origin (Supplementary
Table 13a/b). This grouping is not a per-nucleus genotype assignment. Mixed: the label is
listed as both fetal and maternal. Ambiguous: the label is not listed. Columns show all
nuclei and gestational strata: T1, GW5–13 (9 donors); T2, GW14–27 (9); T3, GW28–40 (5).
Dot area gives the percentage of nuclei with ≥1 UMI. Colour gives the mean
per-nucleus log1p(CP10k) over all nuclei in the stratum (not pseudobulk). Open circles mark strata with ≥20 nuclei and no detected
UMI. Blank positions mark strata with fewer than 20 nuclei. The figure is descriptive: no
statistical test is shown, n is the number of nuclei per stratum, and nuclei from the same
donor are not independent. Donor-level summaries and within-donor contrasts are given in
`tables/expression_by_donor_celltype.csv` and `tables/donor_paired_contrasts.csv`.
Source data: `CP1A_TNFSF12_dotplot_source.csv`.
